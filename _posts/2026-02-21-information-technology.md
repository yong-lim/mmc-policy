excerpt: MMCC members must only use MMCC Information Resources as permitted by this policy. Information and technology associated with or belonging to MMCC must be protected by taking appropriate measures such as keeping passwords private, encrypting all computers and devices, and locking all portable devices. The appropriate use of MMCC Information Resources focuses on two primary areas 1) security and privacy of all hospital information in compliance with any governmental laws/rules or hospital policies (including patient data, financial data, employee information, etc.); 2) individual responsibilities in the use of MMCC resources.
